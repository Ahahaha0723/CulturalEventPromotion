# Toy / Brand Pop-up Fixed Template QA (English)

## Critical
- [ ] Is the event truly a toy / brand pop-up case?
- [ ] Is the fixed template being used instead of a drifting free-form layout?
- [ ] Are real brand / IP names used correctly, or is the concept clearly marked as unofficial?
- [ ] Are fabricated collaborations, gifts, inventory rules, or official claims avoided?
- [ ] Is the top-left block used for date / time / venue?
- [ ] Does the hero match the dominant palette without strong color conflict?

## Major
- [ ] Is the five-zone structure stable?
- [ ] Is the upper-middle title area clearly established?
- [ ] Is the hero large enough and centered in the mid-lower focal area?
- [ ] Is the bottom information panel centered, flat, and readable?
- [ ] Does the bottom panel avoid repeating the top-left time / venue info?
- [ ] Are the Chinese and English layouts separately optimized instead of being mechanically mirrored?

## Minor
- [ ] Is the icon count reasonable?
- [ ] Is there enough contrast between the title zone and the collage wall?
- [ ] Is the small text readable?
- [ ] Are footer notes / concept notices complete?

## Common failures and rollback
| Failure | Roll back to |
|---|---|
| Layout drifts away from the template | template selection |
| Hero clashes with background palette | color scheme / key visual selection |
| Top-left info feels too bold or too large | info hierarchy / typography |
| Bottom panel becomes messy | information panel rules |
| Title looks like plain text instead of a title mark | title area design |
| English version breaks awkwardly | English typography |
