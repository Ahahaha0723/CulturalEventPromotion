# 2027 Goat case

Mechanisms: vermilion/ivory flat profile, heavy curled horn as recognition anchor, eye dissolved into diffuse shadow, selectively blurred outline and static force. A quiet red neck field carries high-contrast serif lettering; two-column footer holds greetings. No lanterns, generic festive icons, cartoon or 3D fabric.

Image prompt: portrait 3:4; warm ivory paper; oversized vermilion ram profile facing right; substantial curled horn with clear negative-space opening; selective defocus on face/body; barely perceptible dark eye patch, no distinct pupil, bright eyeball or jewel highlights; stillness, no running trails; neck creates quiet red typography zone; subtle print echoes, warm haze and grain; planar graphic, no photographed fur, 3D sculpture, letters or logos.

Bodoni Moda is the known headline font from this project. Noto Serif SC substitutes the Chinese Song-style text; Inter substitutes Arial supporting text. Include licenses and do not claim substitutes are the original fonts.

2027 Dingwei/Goat source: https://www.hko.gov.hk/tc/gts/time/calendar/pdf/files/2027.pdf. GRACE IN STILLNESS is a creative adaptation, not an official slogan. The blurred eye avoids anatomical pupil claims. National Day work requires a new motif rather than retaining the ram as a national symbol.
