Avoid unsupported lantern/dragon/cloud decoration, fictional credentials, random language, false brands/QR codes, icon mosaics, busy shelves, glossy products, thick3D shadows, metallic/plastic shine, dark paper and full-image dirt/noise. Final wording is handled through [lettering ink prompts](type-ink-prompts.md) after accurate typesetting.

Use an available image-generation tool and keep the real asset. Coordinates are guidance, not a tool-guaranteed lock.
