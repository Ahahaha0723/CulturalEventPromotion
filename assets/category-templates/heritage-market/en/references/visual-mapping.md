# Mother copy to one coherent visual

Choose supported materials, objects and relationships before prompting. These are conditional examples, not a permanent graphic vocabulary.

| Source content | Available cues | Composition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Independent books/art publications | open pages, spines, folding, binding thread, paper layers | one open book or rhythmic page structure; not a bookstore shelf photograph |
| Dyeing/textiles | dye tones, fabric texture, folds, fibers | restrained printed color within the main object; no invented regional motifs |
| Bamboo/weaving | crossed strips, gaps, curved material | local woven relationships connecting to pages/binding, not a busy full-screen grid |
| Woodblock/printing | cuts, negative space, flat ink marks, block structure | printed edges/tones, not3D glowing embossing |
| Paper craft/bookbinding | folds, paper edges, stitched relationships, small books | link handwork and reading through physical structure |

Paper Encounters may lead with open pages supported by binding thread and woven structure. Use at least two relevant cues while keeping one dominant object. Do not invent official heritage status, ethnic symbols, faces or certificates. A new topic needs a new content-derived image, not the same book graphic with another name. Image assets must actually be generated before final composition.
