# Emblem-derived color and photo mechanism

Use a clear authentic transparent emblem, or cut out its exterior background first. sample_emblem_color.py reports colored-pixel hue candidates, excluding transparent, near-white and low-saturation pixels. It does not certify an official brand standard or automatically select a color. Assess visual prominence as well as filtered pixel share; monochrome emblems may produce no colored candidate. Do not sample a photograph, tinted poster background or reference text as school color evidence.

Use documented official brand values when supplied, otherwise confirm an emblem-sampled reference themeColor. All main and section headings share this color. All body text is exactly #4B4B4B, fully opaque. Do not automatically darken themeColor, add white text inversion or tint gray body copy. If heading contrast is insufficient, clear its backdrop or revise size/weight. Real emblem colors stay intact.

The campus image uses low-contrast monochrome, fine random print grain, light paper fibers and broad fading; a pale version of the confirmed school hue may tint the image. The photo's derived tint is not a new heading color or official palette claim. Read [photo prompts](photo-style-prompts.md), [style reference](../assets/reference/photo-style-reference.jpg) and [cutout rules](emblem-cutout.md). Campus identity comes from the source photo, not the style reference's woodland.

Preserve original roof, columns, arches, windows, ground and trees; enlargement must remain proportional. All four photo edges taper into white, rather than a hard rectangular photo or uniformly reduced opacity. Keep text zones lighter and the emblem free of all texture. No generated text, emblems, historic motifs or QR codes.
