# Composition and expression routes: popular performances

Choose a mechanism from the programme and communication task. Develop two or three relevant alternatives rather than imposing a common neon aesthetic.

|Condition/task|Composition|Chinese typography/information|English recomposition|Profile|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Livehouse: atmosphere/concept|One explainable motif or real live scene; title and motif share focus; date/city remain independent|Compare heavy sans, Song/Ming or authored lettering according to the music; functional copy stays clear|Keep a short title together; group a long title semantically instead of squeezing it|livehouse-atmospheric|
|Festival: lineup|Separate clean lineup and imagery; day/stage modules are independent|Preserve supplied order and billing tiers; compare heavy serif and sans; retain official Latin artist names|Wrap whole artist names; preserve equivalent billing emphasis|festival-lineup|
|Festival: place|Use a meaningful city, terrain or actual performance environment; keep lineup/time separate|City, dates and required lineup remain readable; create schedule detail when necessary|Preserve date ranges and time meaning; do not guess headliners or billed order|festival-lineup|
|Concert/tour: portrait/identity|Approved portrait, official wordmark and tour concept; repeatable city/date module|Manage a new title separately from an official mark; use authentic width variants for long names|Separate artist, tour and city roles; condensed dates do not justify condensed body copy|concert-portrait|

Colour follows programme, artist standards, venue or concept: monochrome grain, one accent, photographic colour and restrained duotones are possible. Texture belongs on permitted image layers. Direction, cutting, rhythm, light or audience space can convey energy without invented stage equipment.

Use independent frames for title, subtitle, lineup, date/city and ticket action. Resolve title-image unity before size hierarchy. Avoid isolated titles, texture-obscured dates and compressed lineup walls. Recompose each aspect ratio; cropping away functional copy is not adaptation.

Prompt plan: performance identity → sourced theme anchor → visual focus → image/type relationship → colour/material logic → five decisions → forbidden inventions. Generate the key visual without formal copy; compose the sourced text using actual font files. Retain approved artwork during typography-only repair.
