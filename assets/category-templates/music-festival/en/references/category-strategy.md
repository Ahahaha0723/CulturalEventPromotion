# Popular live performance: category strategy

Scope: Livehouse, music festivals, concerts/tours and related live music. Communicate who, what experience, when/where and how to attend. Exclude music exhibitions, esports tournaments and product marketing. Genre, act identity, site and programme define expression; a young audience does not automatically call for neon or graffiti.

|Subtype|Leading entry|Attendance information|Risk|
|---|---|---|---|
|Livehouse|Concept/act and specific live mood|Single-show date, venue, doors/show, tickets|Atmosphere without recognizable event format|
|Festival|Identity/lineup and city experience|Date range, day/stage relationships, tiers|A campaign overloaded with a minute-level schedule|
|Tour/concert|Artist and tour identity|City/session, venue, date, on-sale phase|City and title competing; details covering the face|

Define content, audience, action, emotional rhythm, cultural/artist context and viewing channel. Choose one primary and up to two supporting mechanisms: portrait identity, subject/scene, type rhythm, spatial experience or lineup modules. Directions differ by entry and image/type relationships, not recoloring. Do not invent a stage, band member or genre.

The title may lead or follow a portrait/concept object. Respect sourced billing. Keep functional information consistent while allowing mixed title/subtitle fonts; compare optical edges, width, mass and gaps before judging families. Texture must not pass through all required copy.

Read zh-poster-strategy.md and en-poster-strategy.md separately. Chinese may use serif, heavy sans or controlled lettering; English may use broad, condensed or serif routes according to the actual content. Reference wordmarks are assets, not font identifications. Dispatch title, subtitle, body and numeric roles separately.

Create only requested campaign and necessary lineup/day/city/ticketing derivatives. Recompose ratios and inspect mobile previews. Check portrait/logo rights, sessions, tickets, artist spelling and real admission rules before release.
