# Anime convention: character atmosphere and attendance facts

Distinguish a broad anime convention, single-IP exhibition, fan event and market; a character alone does not classify the activity. Require name, dates/daily hours, venue, zones, guests/stages/signing, tiers, official site and actual admission/Cosplay rules. Clarify character/fan-content permissions.

潮流-014 supports image beside modular information with a strong date entry;022 supports a character scene above a separate logo/date band. Do not copy their character, landmark or logo. Profile anime-character compares stable heavy sans and justified rounded/playful alternatives with clear functional text.

Viewers unfamiliar with the characters must still understand event format, date and place. Establish character scale/gaze relationships and protect faces from type. Official lettering is an asset; nearby dates and venue may use different fonts without guessing a commercial font identity from the logo.

Map URLs, tiers, guests and zones across languages. Keep dates and daily hours connected. Check accents and extended Latin place names. Chinese text does not need generic Japanese decoration. Recompose image and modules for landscape rather than shrinking the portrait layout.
