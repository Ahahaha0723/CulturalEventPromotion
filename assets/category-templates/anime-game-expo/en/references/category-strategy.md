# Anime and game exhibitions: category strategy

Scope: anime conventions, game expos and related IP experiences led by viewing, play and exchange. Classify esports by competition and exclude pure product marketing. Establish the primary visitor action in mixed events. Subject and audience decide style; contemporary culture does not imply neon, code or a screen full of characters.
