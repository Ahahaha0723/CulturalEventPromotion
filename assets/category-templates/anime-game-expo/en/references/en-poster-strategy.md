# English-language poster strategy

Use supplied official English titles, artist spellings, programmes and game names. New translations require source and status; do not rename to fill space. Chinese line counts, widths and punctuation do not transfer mechanically.

Serif, broad, condensed and angular headings are conditional choices by subject and role. Check official spelling and readability before all-caps treatment; contemporary culture does not mandate geometric capitals. Protect work names, people, acronyms and tiers. Use actual italic files rather than synthetic slant; expressive italics need a design reason.

## Line grouping and cadence

Group semantics before measuring: protect names, prepositional phrases and meaningful units. Avoid stranded articles/prepositions and unnecessary one-word final lines. Adjust box, size and leading first; choose a secondary semantic break only when needed. Never compress text horizontally to fake condensation. Break a long official multiword name at approved boundaries without dropping words.

Title/format/subtitle/details may contrast in family if character, edges, line groups and image relationships work together. Group multiple times or zones. Do not shrink English solely to match Chinese length. Read connected lines aloud and inspect transitions in line length.

Avoid ambiguous numeric dates; retain the official form or seek approval for a display format. Preserve year, timezone and multi-day meaning. Check accented names. French, Indonesian and Thai reference copy is not automatically the new English master.

Dispatch real files through the English profile; never retype graphic lettering as if it were the original logo. Establish primary/supporting languages for mixed editions without forcing equal sizes. A standalone English poster may need a wider box, different columns and image placement.

Read [case transfer](case-guided-generation.md).022 uses a grouped band with complete event name and date;052 uses actual condensed type with one row or semantic wrapping as needed;053 separates a tracked signature, italic table identity and event mark. Catalan/Spanish/Thai source copy is not the new English master. Do not copy a three-row city split or break words for resemblance.

## Strict font/layout matching

Before generation or text repair, read [best-fitting reference typography](strict-reference-typography.md), inspect images, compare real samples and lock roles/layout. Candidate order is not aesthetic fit; added italics/families or layout changes need source/brief evidence.
