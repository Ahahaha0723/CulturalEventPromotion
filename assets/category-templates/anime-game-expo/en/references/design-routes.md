Distinguish an event key visual, official IP publicity, a creator notice and navigation. Transfer information structure and image/type relationships, not others' characters, marks or proprietary strokes. Do not freely change an unfamiliar character's costume, identity or signs.

Colour follows approved identity, world or theme. Comic panels, halftones, layered depth, interface-like information and spatial nodes are conditional mechanisms. Digital identity does not require neon, glitch or cyber decoration. Depicted gameplay and zone relationships need real evidence.

Connect title to gaze, silhouette and negative space; put body copy on a readable area. Test bright copy on saturated backgrounds. The faint small introduction in014 is not a readability standard. Approved display lettering may overlap; dates, location, ticket categories and table numbers may not.

Wrap Chinese by meaning and protect English brands, names, date ranges and hall numbers. Case052 has Thai text/calendar notation, which does not supply dates for the new event. Use script-compatible fonts and human copy review. French/Spanish references are not purely English posters.

Prompt plan: actual event form → authorised/original asset inventory → character/gameplay/zone anchor → image/type priorities → colour/space logic → formal copy space. Validate each scenario separately; a booth notice, themed experience or navigation graphic does not prove a general game-expo key visual works.

## Concrete three-case mechanisms

Use [case transfer](case-guided-generation.md):022 ensemble/city and an independent information band;052 portrait/torn materials and a huge condensed headline;053 narrative illustration/signature and table identity. Assign image, style and typography roles after identifying the event format; do not mechanically merge the three structures.

## Strict font/layout matching

Before generation or text repair, read [best-fitting reference typography](strict-reference-typography.md), inspect images, compare real samples and lock roles/layout. Candidate order is not aesthetic fit; added italics/families or layout changes need source/brief evidence.
