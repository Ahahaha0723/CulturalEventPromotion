# Reference mechanisms and adaptation

Unseen Realms combines silver-grey abstract flow, near-black bold sans headlines, offset lines, sparse fluorescent lime, white information plates, dashed guides and square endpoints. Lime marks entry points and visual focus. The slashed circle is a case-specific perception motif, not a default symbol for all exhibitions.

Noto Sans SC handles Chinese; Inter handles English and numerals. Use weight contrast. English title lines retain the offset rhythm; the subtitle may wrap to protect the symbol. Painting can substitute artwork crops and pigment accents; photography can use an image-led limited palette and fewer symbols; digital art can use data texture, disruption, grids or sequences. Select mechanisms from the artist's work instead of enforcing silver water texture universally.

Case-specific background prompt: portrait 3:4, cool silver grey/white abstract data landscape, horizontal ripples and vertical disruptions, fine dense details with quiet upper/lower text zones, planar texture, no letters/logos/exhibition-room photography.
