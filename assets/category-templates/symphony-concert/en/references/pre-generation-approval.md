# Five pre-generation decisions

Before the first key-visual generation, present a completed, readable decision table to the user. A silent internal pass is insufficient. Reuse explicit earlier decisions and identify the supporting reply/document. A general request to “test the result” does not confirm every item.

|Decision|Show|States|
|---|---|---|
|Direction|Design proposition, reading entry, image/type relationship, selected subtype strategy|confirmed / pending|
|Cultural or identity elements|Actual artists, programme, opera tradition, works or characters with evidence; explicitly note when absent|confirmed / pending / not-applicable|
|Asset permissions|Identity, provider, permitted use and unknown rights|confirmed / pending|
|Canvas|Ratio, pixels or vendor print specification, quantities, languages, channels|confirmed / pending|
|Formal copy space|Reserved areas for title, format, lineup/programme/zones, dates, venue and CTA; long-copy treatment|confirmed / pending|

Inherit unchanged approvals without asking again. Reconfirm only materially changed direction, core identity assets or output scope. Explicit quick-draft or delegated design authorization permits exploration within its scope; it does not establish unknown rights, official translations or historical identity. If no direction is authorized and the choice materially affects generation, offer concrete options and await the answer while completing independent intake, font specimens and fact mapping.

Record exploratory and final-release authorization separately. Essential unresolved facts or core asset permissions block a final version. Never present an exploratory scene as documentary evidence, an official work or authorized IP. Save assets/pre-generation-review-template.json with values, states, sources, version scope and remaining issues. Model self-review is not human confirmation.
