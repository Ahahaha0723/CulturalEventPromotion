# Traditional and classical performance: transfer priorities

Use with case-evidence.md, design-routes.md and the actual subtype. Learn program narrative, cultural accuracy and type character; do not inherit Livehouse monochrome, spikes or record imagery.

- Case053 links light lettering, coastal space and linear musical voices. Adapt to this program's structure and negative space; use semantic English phrases instead of copying decorative split letters.
- Case065 links rhythmic line networks with separate vertical Chinese and English modules. Decide vertical writing and graphic voices from this program; fusion music is not Chinese-opera or Xiangsheng evidence.
- Case109 offers adjacent evidence for silhouette, theatrical color and written-title relationships. It remains a dance reference, not an opera exemplar or permission to copy calligraphy and characters.

For opera, anchor decisions in the actual play, role, costume and movement; for Xiangsheng, the duo and verbal rhythm; for symphony, the works, ensemble and performance character. Compare real-copy specimens before selecting serif, sans or original title construction. Do not make every traditional event generic antiquity. Direct opera/Xiangsheng exemplars are absent: retain pending-case. Inspect and record new supplied images before claiming exemplar evidence.
