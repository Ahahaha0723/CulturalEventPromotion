## Case053: coastal “Flowing Light”
Bundled original: assets/reference-posters/P609.jpg; a high-consensus orchestral reference. Exact source font families remain unknown.
- **Key-visual effect:** blue-gray coastal photography supplies depth; white diagonal surf leads the eye, dark beach supports information, and open upper space lets the heading breathe.
- **Visual style:** muted slate blue, silver-white type, fine serifs and photographic restraint. Contrast concentrates in lettering, surf and dark ground.
- **Typography:** large Chinese title groups occupy both upper sides around a small date. Staggered italic fragments connect to many horizontal five-line groups, with varied length, tracking and starts; year and month/day join the rhythm. Lower-left information and a right mark finish the page.
- **Adaptation:** borrow space, direction and image/type rhythm, not a mandatory coast. Compose complete words/phrases across multiple rows with sourced date nodes where suitable. Do not default to copied broken words. Preserve the full formal title and unambiguous complete date in a legible information area; determine row count and scale from copy and canvas.

## Case065: “Mountains and Sea · DaGuan”
Bundled original: assets/reference-posters/P630.jpg; high-consensus Chinese/fusion music evidence, adjacent to symphony and not direct opera/Xiangsheng evidence.
- **Key-visual effect:** alternating vertical and diagonal fine-line bundles create the main image; varied lengths, density and gaps suggest rhythm and musical voices.
- **Visual style:** black ground, gray-white and subdued green lines; restrained planar geometry with fine texture and void.
- **Typography:** vertical Chinese on the right establishes the axis, with vertical supporting copy at left; English and date/time modules enter gaps at different positions and scales.
- **Adaptation:** borrow line rhythm and embedded text relationships. Decide vertical writing from this actual title, language and format, not the reference alone. Do not import its people, identity or dates.

## Combining the two
Assign different roles before building a coherent proposition. In the film-score test,053 supplies blue-gray atmosphere, depth, space and light typography;065 supplies line direction, density and embedded text. This programme's silver-screen light and orchestral rhythm connect them into original imagery. Do not paste incompatible layouts together.
Image, mood and typography references may differ. The user explicitly chose053 for the later typography;065 does not automatically require vertical writing.

## Multiple rows, dates and natural proportions
For a053-like central rhythm, prepare readable groups and optionally add sourced year, month/day and performance-time nodes. Vary lengths, starts, tracking and intervals around image direction. Four or nine rows, blue, italics and staffs are not universal requirements.
Derive dates only from the content master. Decorative reformatting does not replace the complete functional date. Clarify month/day or use a named month when numeric dates are ambiguous. Record display case/punctuation separately from the formal name.
Chinese/English share the proposition but may use different title sizes and groups. Load real font binaries and true italic files; change tracking without stretching/squeezing glyphs. A standalone short word may serve an explicitly approved decorative rhythm, while the formal title and continuous sentences remain semantically readable.

## Subtype boundaries
- Chinese opera: anchor in the actual genre, play, role, costume and movement. Borrow focus, space and type/image relationships; abstract lines need a movement or vocal rationale, not copied cinema beams. Preserve casting and roles.
- Xiangsheng: anchor in the show proposition, duo, pauses and dialogue. Borrow text rhythm/modules while preserving names, pairings and sessions; abstraction must not erase the duo relationship.
- Symphony: choose imagery/graphics from works, ensemble, performance character or film-score proposition. Lines may express voices and imagery may express emotion; coasts, gold instruments and stages are not defaults.
Direct opera/Xiangsheng exemplars remain pending-case; this iteration tests symphony reference transfer, not audience success for those other forms.
